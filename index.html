01 / ABOUT

A curiosity for
how we move.

Every movement is a conversation between the brain, the body and the world around us.

My research examines the neural, cognitive and biomechanical mechanisms of human motor control and learning, with a particular focus on sensorimotor adaptation. I’m interested in how the brain responds to change, and how that knowledge can inform motor rehabilitation.

I hold a PhD in Sensorimotor Neuroscience from The University of Queensland. My academic journey has also taken me to Princeton University for postdoctoral research and Macquarie University, where I lectured in motor control and learning.
